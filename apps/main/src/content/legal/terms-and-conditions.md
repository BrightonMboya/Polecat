## Booking tours

### A. Making a booking

Booking a safari, Zanzibar extension or Kilimanjaro climb is usually an interactive process. Once you've told us what you have in mind, we'll go back and forth with you on the itinerary and the recommended package. This is normal, and it makes sure you book the right trip for you. Nothing is booked or charged until you say yes to an itinerary.

Once this process is complete and you're ready to go ahead with a quote we've agreed on, we'll send payment details so you can pay a deposit, and then send confirmation of the trip.

We'll then send you forms to fill in so we can collect details such as inbound flights and dietary needs. Our Registration Form allows certain limited special requests, such as dietary needs, to be written into the contract. We will do our best to honour these requests, and will tell you in advance if we can't.

However, if a request is made that is not part of our Registration Form, it does not form part of the contract, whatever other communication may have taken place outside the trip documents. At any time between the making of a booking and the delivery of receipt paperwork to the client or client representative, the Company reserves the right to decline to supply the trip.

### B. Price guarantee

Once you've made your booking and we've received your deposit, we guarantee to honour the prices for each individual item shown on your quotation, with the following exceptions:

- **Estimated price increases**: where we are booking for a period for which we do not yet have prices from the supplier (for example a year in advance of the trip date), we usually include an estimated price increase. This is reassessed by the time final payment is due, and the client or client representative must cover such increases up to a maximum of twice the estimated increase.
- **Government-regulated fees and taxes**: in Tanzania, we are exposed to unannounced and sudden changes in items such as national park fees, Kilimanjaro park fees and taxes. The client or client representative is responsible for paying any additional charges of this kind.
- **Fuel surcharges**: we are also occasionally exposed to sudden changes in fuel costs, which we must pass on if our suppliers charge them to us. The client or client representative is responsible for paying any additional charges of this kind.
- **Major errors**: if there is a major error in our quotation and we have significantly undercharged for a trip, we reserve the right to withdraw our offer.
- **Exchange rate fluctuations**: very occasionally, our suppliers face a large movement in international exchange rates and add an exchange rate surcharge to their invoices to us. The client or client representative is responsible for paying any additional charges of this kind.

### Payment terms

Payments are scheduled as follows:

- Deposit due on camping safari bookings: 10%
- Deposit due on lodge safari bookings: 40%
- Deposit due on tailor-made bookings: 10%–70%, on request
- Final payment is due on arrival (cash accepted and preferred)

Payment can be made by bank wire/transfer, credit card, or online payment (e.g. PesaPal). We will provide our banking details to make this possible.

All payments should be made to company accounts. All company accounts are held in Tanzania; we do not hold any offshore accounts.

We often receive separate payments from different people in a group travelling together, and we accept payments from each of them (a payment link will be sent to each person to make this easier). However, the relevant information should always be passed on through the group leader, who remains financially responsible for the whole trip.

We strongly recommend all clients take out insurance cover for such events.

Sometimes we are unable to book the trip exactly as quoted, often because a lodge has no rooms available, or because of a serious issue such as a lodge or road being damaged by fire or flooding. If this happens, all payment obligations for the affected element are waived. We do, however, reserve the right to make small modifications during the booking process that have no impact on the contract or on the client's payment obligations. - Adjusting the number of nights spent at each lodge/hotel

## Cancellation

Cancelling less than 10 days before the trip forfeits the full trip fee. A refund may be considered in extraordinary, provable circumstances.

Cancelling more than 10 days before the trip date will result in a refund, less bank transaction costs (lodge-based tours are the exception, and follow the lodge's own cancellation policy).

Exchanges are allowed; no refunds are given for cancelled individual tours. Full travel and flight insurance is required to protect you in the event of a cancellation or change. In some cases, most often bookings made in shoulder season, a credit note against the deposit paid may be offered instead.
